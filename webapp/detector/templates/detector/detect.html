How the decision is made

  1. Resize the photo to 224 × 224 RGB. Pixel values stay in 0–255, the range the published weights expect.
  2. MobileNet (ImageNet features) plus a small trained head scores the ten State Farm activities.
  3. Decide: if the top activity is anything other than c0 safe driving, the driver is flagged as distracted.
  4. Alert: the app sounds an alarm. Here you see the verdict, the confidence and the runner-up activities.

Read the result with care

  • The sample frames are small thumbnails (about 200 × 150 px) cut from the dataset's preview image, so they are blurrier than real input. Half of them come out as "operating the radio", a sign that small, blurry input hurts. The thumbnails have no ground-truth label, so they are a demo, not a test.
  • The model only knows the State Farm set-up: one camera angle, daylight, left-hand-drive cars. Photos from other angles, at night, or from right-hand-drive cars (as in Indonesia) are outside what it learned.
  • The 98% validation score shares drivers with the training data, so expect lower accuracy on new people. See the evaluation →
