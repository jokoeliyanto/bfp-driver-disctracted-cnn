Presentation Overview

  • Problem Statement: Detect distracted driving in real time on a phone without internet connection.
  • Solution: Compact CNN using MobileNet transfer learning deployed as TensorFlow Lite on Android.
  • Results: High recall on distraction detection with low latency suitable for mobile deployment.
  • Impact: Named one of the four best final projects of Bangkit 2020 cohort by Google Indonesia.
  • Future Work: Right-hand-drive vehicle adaptation, driver-split evaluation, and temporal smoothing.
